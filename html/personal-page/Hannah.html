My name is Hannah Rohringer. I am 21 years old and I am originally from Brazil. I moved to Norway back in 2023, right after I finished high school. Norwegian is my third language so I avoid writing in Norwegian if I can. Learning Norwegian was definitely a challenge, I am still not very good at it, good enough to get into uni at least.
I try dedicating my free time to my hobbies. That includes gamming, crochet, drawing and painting. I just recently started selling my crochet projects, if you are really curious about it, you can find my Instagram by clicking the byRohri bellow. You can also find them at a local tattoo studio called The Hideout located in Fyllingsdalen. It is a very cosy studio. The owner is very talented and friendly. If you are thinking on getting a tattoo, I would totally recommend checking it out.
Programming isn’t exactly my dream area, but I grew up with people around me that works in the area so I have a lot of extra support and help when needed, especially since I have the extra language barrier. It is also able to provide more stability and freedom, more job options, being able to work long distance, if AI doesn’t take over all of it by the time I graduate LOL. My dream job would be something leaning towards art, like animation and stuff. It seems very fun bringing life to little ideas that pops to mind. But maybe one day when I have a bit more time and flexibility, I might be able to dive in that area.
Also meet Toffee. He was my favorite creature in the world, unfortunatly he past in 2021. He was a pomeraninan with a mutt soul, had secret adventures of his own. I would write about them, but well they are a mystery.
